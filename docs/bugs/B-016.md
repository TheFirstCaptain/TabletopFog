# B-016: Handout Shown Action Spacing Shifts

## Summary

Handout card action spacing changes when a handout becomes `Shown to Players`,
making the shown card's action row sit closer to its title than neighboring
unshown cards.

## Status

Resolved.

## Observed Behavior

After clicking `Show to Players` for a handout, the card changes to
`Shown to Players` with inline rotation buttons, but the vertical space between
the handout title and the action row is smaller than the same space on unshown
handout cards.

## Expected Behavior

Shown and unshown Handout cards should keep consistent title-to-action spacing
so changing the shown handout state does not disturb the gallery rhythm.

## Reproduction

1. Open a campaign with at least three campaign Handouts.
2. Switch to `Handouts`.
3. Click `Show to Players` on one Handout card.
4. Compare the vertical space between the title and action row on the shown
   card against neighboring unshown cards.

## Affected Systems

- GM view: Handout card layout.
- Player view: unchanged.
- Local server: unchanged.
- Map/fog rendering: unchanged.
- Storage: unchanged.
- Network/device setup: unchanged.
- Documentation: bug tracker and F-010H follow-up note.

## Investigation Notes

- The issue was reported by the user with a screenshot on 2026-08-01 after the
  B-015 inline rotation control fix.
- Handout cards are CSS grids stretched to match the tallest card in a gallery
  row. Without explicit content alignment, shorter cards can distribute extra
  vertical space across internal tracks, changing title-to-action spacing.
