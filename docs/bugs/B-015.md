# B-015: Shown Handout Rotation Buttons Misalign Cards

## Summary

Shown Handout cards place rotation controls on a second action row, making the
shown card taller than neighboring Handout cards.

## Status

Resolved.

## Observed Behavior

When a Handout is shown to players, `Rotate left` and `Rotate right` buttons
appear below the `Shown to Players` action. This pushes the shown Handout card
content downward while unshown Handout cards keep a single action row.

## Expected Behavior

The shown Handout card should keep rotation controls inline with the
`Shown to Players` action as compact left-90 and right-90 controls, preserving
the Handout gallery card rhythm.

## Reproduction

1. Open a campaign with at least three campaign Handouts.
2. Switch to `Handouts`.
3. Show one Handout to players.
4. Observe the shown Handout card's rotation controls appearing below the
   `Shown to Players` action and pushing lower card content out of alignment
   with neighboring cards.

## Affected Systems

- GM view: Handout card action layout.
- Player view: unchanged.
- Local server: unchanged.
- Map/fog rendering: unchanged.
- Storage: unchanged.
- Network/device setup: unchanged.
- Documentation: bug tracker and F-010H follow-up note.

## Investigation Notes

- The issue was reported by the user with a screenshot on 2026-08-01 during
  F-010H validation.
- The current GM renderer appends a nested `.handout-rotation-controls` flex
  row after the shown-state button, so rotation controls render as a separate
  group inside the card action area.

## Validation Plan

Browser regression coverage verifies the shown Handout rotate controls are
compact icon-style buttons in the same row as the `Shown to Players` action,
and that rotating still updates the Player Display without exposing player-side
mutation controls.
