# F-011A: Campaign-Specific Waiting State

## Objective

Show campaign-specific waiting text on the Player Display while a campaign is
open and no encounter or handout is currently shown to players.

## User Value

The always-on Player Display can identify the current campaign between table
moments without requiring the GM to show an encounter, handout, or image.

## Non-Goals

- Do not add Campaign Image upload, replacement, removal, storage, or rendering
  in this slice.
- Do not add slideshow mode.
- Do not add campaign notes, session notes, text notes, or handout notes.
- Do not make opening or editing an encounter change the Player Display.
- Do not add tokens, initiative, dice, character sheets, rules automation,
  dynamic lighting, cloud hosting, login, or authentication.

## Acceptance Notes

- When no campaign is open, the Player Display keeps the current plain
  `Waiting for GM.` state.
- When a campaign is open and no encounter or handout is shown to players, the
  Player Display shows `Waiting for GM - <Campaign Name>`.
- Showing an encounter replaces the campaign waiting state.
- Showing a handout replaces the campaign waiting state.
- Clearing the shown encounter or handout returns to
  `Waiting for GM - <Campaign Name>` for the open campaign.
- Opening, editing, or selecting an encounter in the GM View does not change
  the Player Display.
- Browsing handouts in the GM View does not change the Player Display.
- The Player Display remains read-only.
