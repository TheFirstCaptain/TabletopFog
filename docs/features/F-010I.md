# F-010I: GM Handout Rotation Preview

## Objective

When a handout is shown to players and rotated, rotate the handout preview in
the GM View so it matches what the Player Display shows.

## User Value

The GM can trust the campaign Handouts view as an accurate preview of the
player-facing display. When the GM rotates a shown handout, they do not need to
look at the Player Display to confirm orientation.

## Non-Goals

- Do not add player-side rotation.
- Do not add arbitrary-angle rotation.
- Do not change rotation persistence, storage shape, server routes, Socket.IO
  synchronization, or Player Display projection.
- Do not rotate unshown handout library metadata.
- Do not rotate encounter maps or fog.
- Do not add crop, flip, brightness, annotation, image editing, slideshow
  behavior, folders, tags, or search.
- Do not change handout upload, rename, delete, recovery, or shown-target clear
  behavior.
- Do not add tokens, initiative, dice, notes, character sheets, rules
  automation, dynamic lighting, cloud hosting, login, or authentication.

## Acceptance Notes

- When a handout is currently shown to players, its GM View card preview uses
  the same quarter-turn rotation as the Player Display.
- Clicking rotate-left or rotate-right updates both the Player Display and the
  GM View card preview.
- The GM View preview supports only the existing `0`, `90`, `180`, and `270`
  degree rotation values.
- The rotated GM View preview remains contained inside the existing card image
  area without cropping important content, overflowing the card, or shifting
  unrelated controls.
- Unshown handout cards remain unrotated in the library.
- Showing a handout starts its GM View preview at `0` degrees, matching the
  existing shown-display rotation behavior.
- Reopening or reloading the campaign restores the GM View preview rotation for
  the currently shown handout.
- Showing an encounter after a rotated handout clears handout rotation from the
  GM View preview, matching the Player Display.
- Player-side read-only guarantees are unchanged.
