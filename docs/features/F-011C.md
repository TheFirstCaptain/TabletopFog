# F-011C: Campaign Waiting Image Display

## Objective

Render the optional Campaign Image as the Player Display background while the
campaign waiting screen is active.

## User Value

The always-on Player Display can show campaign atmosphere between encounters or
handouts while still making it clear that the GM has not currently shown a
specific table asset.

## Non-Goals

- Do not add Campaign Image upload, replacement, removal, or storage in this
  slice; build on [F-011B](./F-011B.md).
- Do not add slideshow mode.
- Do not add multiple Campaign Images.
- Do not add crop, rotation, filters, positioning controls, or image editing.
- Do not add text notes, campaign notes, session notes, video, audio, PDF, or
  non-image waiting-screen media.
- Do not let the Player Display choose, upload, replace, remove, or mutate the
  Campaign Image.

## Acceptance Notes

- When a campaign is open, no encounter or handout is shown to players, and a
  Campaign Image exists, the Player Display uses it as a background with
  `Waiting for GM - <Campaign Name>` over it.
- If that campaign has no Campaign Image, the Player Display still shows
  `Waiting for GM - <Campaign Name>` without a background image.
- Showing an encounter replaces the campaign waiting image display.
- Showing a handout replaces the campaign waiting image display.
- Clearing the shown encounter or handout returns to the campaign waiting image
  display for the open campaign.
- Uploading, replacing, or removing the Campaign Image updates the Player
  Display live when players are currently on the campaign waiting screen.
- Uploading, replacing, or removing the Campaign Image does not change the
  Player Display while an encounter or handout is currently shown to players.
- Missing or invalid Campaign Image assets fail to the campaign waiting text
  without the background image and report useful GM-only diagnostics.
- The Player Display remains read-only.

  the campaign waiting screen without changing map, fog, zoom, pan, fullscreen,
