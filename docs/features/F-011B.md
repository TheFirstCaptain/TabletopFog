# F-011B: Campaign Image Management

## Objective

Allow the GM to upload, replace, and remove one campaign-level Campaign Image
from the create/edit campaign flow.

## User Value

The GM can prepare campaign atmosphere for the Player Display waiting screen
without creating an encounter map or campaign handout.

## Non-Goals

- Do not render the Campaign Image on the Player Display in this slice; track
  that as [F-011C](./F-011C.md).
- Do not add multiple Campaign Images.
- Do not add slideshow mode.
- Do not add crop, rotation, filters, positioning controls, or image editing.
- Do not add text notes, campaign notes, session notes, video, audio, PDF, or
  non-image waiting-screen media.
- Do not let the Player Display upload, replace, remove, or mutate the Campaign
  Image.

## Acceptance Notes

- The GM can upload one Campaign Image from the create/edit campaign flow.
- The GM can replace the existing Campaign Image from the create/edit campaign
  flow.
- The GM can remove the existing Campaign Image from the create/edit campaign
  flow.
- The Campaign Image uses the same supported image inputs and validation
  expectations as map and handout upload unless implementation review finds a
  documented reason to differ.
- Invalid, mismatched, oversized, partial-failure, missing-asset, unsafe-path,
  and symlink-escape cases leave existing campaign data and assets unchanged
  where practical.
- Campaign Image metadata is stored at campaign scope and does not create an
  encounter or handout.
- Uploading, replacing, or removing the Campaign Image does not change the
  Player Display while an encounter or handout is currently shown to players.
- The Player Display remains read-only.
