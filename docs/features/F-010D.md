# F-010D: Handout Display Polish and Validation

## Objective

Polish the completed handout workflow and validate it as a coherent
table-ready feature.

## User Value

The GM can rely on handouts during a session without confusing players, losing
the current display state, or exposing unwanted controls.

## Non-Goals

- Do not add new handout media types.
- Do not add folders, tags, search, notes, annotations, or slideshow mode.
- Do not add overlay mode.
- Do not add player-side rotation.
- Do not add unrelated encounter, map, or fog polish.

## Acceptance Notes

- The GM can clearly tell whether players are seeing nothing, an encounter, or
  a handout from the Campaign screen, regardless of whether the GM is viewing
  `Encounters` or `Handouts`.
- Showing an encounter after a handout and showing a handout after an encounter
  are both predictable.
- Handout image loading, error, reconnect, reload, and fullscreen behavior are
  validated across representative viewports.
- Player Display exposes no shared-state mutation controls.
- Existing encounter map and fog workflows remain unchanged.
- README, roadmap, acceptance tests, and development documentation are swept
  for stale user-facing workflow language.
